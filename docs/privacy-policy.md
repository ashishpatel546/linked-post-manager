AppmeSoft Private Limited ("AppmeSoft", "we", "our") operates the website
[www.appme.in](https://www.appme.in) and the software described below. This
policy explains what information we handle and how.

## 1. Our website

We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to
browse www.appme.in. If you contact us through a form or by email, we receive
what you choose to send — typically your name, email address, and your message —
and we use it only to reply to you and to keep a record of the enquiry.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for
advertising.

## 2. LinkedIn Posting Assistant

We operate an internal tool that publishes content to AppmeSoft's own LinkedIn
presence: our company page and the personal profile of the authorised employee
who connects it. The tool is not offered to the public and is used only by
authorised AppmeSoft staff acting on our own accounts.

### What it accesses

With the explicit consent of the LinkedIn member who authorises it, and only
through LinkedIn's official APIs, the tool accesses:

- **Basic profile information** — name, LinkedIn member identifier, profile
  picture, and email address, via Sign In with LinkedIn using OpenID Connect.
  Used solely to confirm which account is connected and to attribute posts to the
  correct author.
- **Permission to publish** — the ability to create and delete posts on the
  authorising member's profile and on our company page.
- **Our own company page content and statistics** — posts, the comments left on
  them, and aggregate engagement and follower figures for our page. Used only to
  understand how our own content performs.

The tool does **not** access the member's LinkedIn feed, connections, or
messages, and does not collect information about other LinkedIn members beyond
comments those members have chosen to leave publicly on our own posts.

### How the data is handled

- The OAuth access token is stored locally on the authorised operator's own
  computer, in a file restricted to that operating-system user. It is not
  transmitted to AppmeSoft servers or to any third party.
- Post drafts and a record of what was published are stored as local files on
  that same computer.
- The tool has no hosted backend, no database, and no analytics or telemetry. It
  transmits data only to LinkedIn's own APIs.

### Content generation

Drafts may be prepared with the assistance of an AI model. Only the topic and
writing instructions typed by the operator are sent to that model. Personal data
retrieved from LinkedIn is never sent to any AI provider. Every draft is reviewed
and explicitly approved by a person before anything is published.

### What we do not do

- We do not sell, rent, or share LinkedIn data with third parties.
- We do not use LinkedIn data for advertising, profiling, or training machine
  learning models.
- We do not scrape LinkedIn or access any data outside the documented LinkedIn
  APIs and the permissions granted through OAuth.

### Retention and revocation

The access token is deleted when the operator removes the local token file, and
in any case expires within 60 days. The authorising member may revoke access at
any time from LinkedIn's **Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services**, which
immediately ends the tool's ability to act on their behalf. Posts published
through the tool can be deleted like any other LinkedIn post.
